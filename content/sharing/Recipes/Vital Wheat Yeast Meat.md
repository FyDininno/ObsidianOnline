# Optimum Seitan Formula

> Based on the “25% nooch” ratio highlighted in the video as a natural dough relaxer ([youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?pp=0gcJCf0Ao7VqN5tD&v=4DSZd1zrp3Y&utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

## Dry Ingredients

|Ingredient|Amount|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|Vital Wheat Gluten|630 g|75 % of total dry weight|
|Nutritional Yeast|210 g|25 % of total dry weight|
|Garlic Powder|5 g|~1 tsp|
|Ground Black Pepper|2 g|~½ tsp|
|Umami Seasoning|10 g|Trader Joe’s or equivalent|
|Salt|5 g|Adjust to taste|

## Wet Ingredients

- Low-Sodium Soy Sauce: 120 mL (~½ cup)
    
- Liquid Smoke: 5 mL (~1 tsp)
    
- Water: 200–250 mL (enough to bring dough together)
    

## Optional Additions

- **Chickpea Flour**: 1/3 cup per 1 1/3 cup VWG for a softer texture (Choline abundant)
    
- **Oil (e.g., neutral vegetable oil)**: 1 Tbsp for extra moisture
    

---

# Cooking Instructions

1. **Combine Dry Ingredients**: In a large bowl, whisk together vital wheat gluten, nutritional yeast, garlic powder, pepper, umami seasoning, and salt.
    
2. **Add Wet Ingredients**: Stir in soy sauce and liquid smoke.
    
3. **Form Dough**: Gradually add water, mixing until a shaggy dough forms. It should hold together without being too sticky.
    
4. **Knead Lightly**: Knead for 1–2 minutes until elastic but avoid overworking.
    
5. **Shape**: Form into a log or portion into chunks as desired.
    
6. **Bake**:
    
    - Preheat oven to 220 °C (420 °F).
        
    - Place dough on a lightly oiled baking sheet.
        
    - Bake for 15–20 minutes, then flip and bake for another 10 minutes until golden and firm ([reddit.com](https://www.reddit.com/r/veganrecipes/comments/1lq2y63/make_your_own_seitan_at_home_too/)).
        
7. **Rest and Slice**: Let cool 5 minutes, then slice or pull into pieces.
    

---

## Storage

- **Refrigerate**: Up to 7 days in an airtight container.
    
- **Freeze**: Up to 3 months; thaw in fridge before use.
    

Enjoy your optimally textured, high‑protein seitan in stir‑fries, sandwiches, or any recipe calling for a meaty vegan substitute!

## Why These Ratios

- **75% Vital Wheat Gluten / 25% Nutritional Yeast**: Balances elasticity and tenderness. VWG forms the gluten network for a meaty chew, while nutritional yeast acts as a natural dough relaxer—softening the structure and imparting savory, umami-rich flavor.
    
- **Dry to Wet Ratio (~1 : 0.35)**: Provides just enough hydration to fully activate gluten without creating a sticky dough. This ratio ensures structural integrity so the seitan holds shape during baking or simmering.
    
- **Soy Sauce (~15% of Wet Weight)**: Contributes both moisture and umami salts, enhancing gluten development and depth of flavor without oversaturating the dough.
    
- **Liquid Smoke (<1% of Wet Weight)**: Delivers smoky aroma in trace amounts, preventing an overt “smoke bomb” while still mimicking char-grilled notes.
    

These proportions are optimized to yield seitan that is springy yet tender, richly flavored, and high in protein—perfect for replicating the texture and mouthfeel of meat in vegan dishes.

### 📋 Notes & Tips

- **99 Ranch extra caution**: their loose‑barrel mushrooms can carry residues—opt instead for certified‑organic dried shiitake.  
- **Bulk vs. packaged**: if you buy nuts in bulk, use your own glass containers to reduce plastic.  
- **Storage**: transfer powders (kale, spinach, parsley) into airtight glass jars to preserve nutrients and avoid light exposure.  
- **Supplements**: keep choline and yeast in cool, dry place; check expiration.  
- **Online vendors** like Nuts.com and Wildly Organic provide **eco-friendly packaging** and broader ingredient options than supermarkets.

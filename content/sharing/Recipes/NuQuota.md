Cooking instructions:

*“Legume Lagoon”*
1. The night before, add 10 cups of water to a large cooking pot - at least 8 quarts large - and toss in the chickpeas (to soak them overnight).
2. In the morning, bring the pot to a boil and toss in the green peas (if using them). Boil them for 30 minutes.
3. Toss in the lentils, the split peas, and mung beans (if you’re using them). Boil them another 15 min.
4. Toss in the carrots. Boil them for another 5 min.
5. Add the clams. If you would like, you may now add in the mushrooms. I usually boil up some water in a kettle and a make separate mushroom stew. Boil them for another 10 min. 
6. Serve with nutritional yeast, parsley, and olive oil.

*“Plenty of Cobbler at the Bottom”*
1. The night before, get a mug and fill it with 1/2c of rolled oats.
2. Add 1/4c of chia seeds.
3. Add anything else you want like fruits, syrup, or cinnamon powder. Save the nuts until the morning.
4. Fill the mug with 1/2c almond milk.
5. Cap the mug and let it sit overnight.
6. In the morning, crush up some nuts and sprinkle them over the top.

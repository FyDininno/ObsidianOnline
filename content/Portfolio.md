# Node Form
One project I am proud of is the "Node Form" project. During my freshman year of college, I was inspired by a math textbook about multivariable calculus. After seeing one of the transformations play out in my head, I was determined to give other people the ability to see what I saw through code. After many late nights staying up and learning python from chatgpt, I finished my first version of the program. It was not until nearly a year later when I completely finalized my project. The results of which can be found here on [github](https://github.com/FyDininno/NodeForm), and the tutorial for it is [[Tutorial|here]].

# Optical Waveguide Profiles
I use use FEnICsx, an open source finite element package, to help my lab to understand the normal modes of their optical waveguides. In the summer of 2024, my principle investigator wanted me to explore open source finite element packages for the use of optical simulations. To complete the task, I learned the mathematics behind various finite element methods and applied my knowledge to successfully solve for normal modes of waveguides with arbitrary 2D crossections. My next project is to simulate electromagnetic resonators with arbitrary geometries.

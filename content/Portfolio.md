# Optical Waveguide Profiles
I use use FEnICsx, an open source finite element package, to help my lab to understand the normal modes of their optical waveguides. I learned the mathematics behind the finite element method and applied my knowledge to successfully solve for normal modes of waveguides with arbitrary 2D crossections. My next project is to simulate electromagnetic resonators.

# Optical Waveguide Profiles
I use use FEnICsx, an open source finite element package, to help my lab to understand the normal modes of their system. I was assigned this task to phase out our use of Lumerical, whose prices were becoming unpredictable. To complete this task, I learned the mathematics behind the finite element method and applied my knowledge to successfully solve for normal modes of waveguides. My next project is to simulate electromagnetic resonators.

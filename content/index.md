**Autobiography**

---
*Early Life*
I am a California-born US citizen. My life since childhood has been focused around science, mathematics, and engineering, driven by genuine curiosity and the will to succeed academically. Four major extracurricular achievements are notable in my life before college. I was in the Boy Scouts, a community service organization in the U.S., and I achieved the highest honor of the organization, the Eagle Scout rank. Second, I participated in a Martial Arts organization for nine years and earned the title of the Black Belt. Third, I was a CAD leader in my high school FIRST robotics team, and our team advanced to the world championships. Finally, I excelled in honors calculus classes throughout my high school and gave lectures and proofs of important theorems such as Green's Theorem and Gauss' Theorem. 

---
*University*
After high school, I was accepted to The University of California, San Diego to pursue a degree in Nanoengineering. I chose this major because I wanted to know how matter behaved, and quantum mechanics was a central concentration in the curriculum. Throughout the curriculum I have maintained a high GPA and have taken technical classes outside of the major, such as those in the chemistry, electrical engineering, physics, and mathematics departments. My overall greatest achievement during my college years was the manufacturing of a graphene compression device to explore the thermal properties of graphene composites. Overall benchmarking tests showed that compressed composites displayed better thermal properties than pure graphite alone.

---
*Laboratory*
I joined a photonics laboratory at UCSD during the summer of 2024. I have been cleanroom-trained and have aided graduate students to develop recipes for etching waveguides. I have consistently attended group meetings for one and a half years and my knowledge of photonics has been greatly increased. My future endeavor is to develop new on-chip semiconductor light sources and novel photonic modulators and receivers.
